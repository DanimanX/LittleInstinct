Killer Instinct / Killer Instinct 2 for the Analogue Pocket.

Port of the MiSTer core Arcade-KillerInstinct_MiSTer (Midway KI hardware:
R4600 MIPS III + DCS audio + ATA hard disk).

STATUS: BRING-UP MILESTONE 1. This does not play the games.

What works, verified in Quartus 25.1 for the Pocket's 5CEBA4F23C8:
  - the Quartus project builds and fits (456 ALMs, 224/224 pins), producing
    pocket_ki.rbf
  - PLL: 74.25 MHz reference -> 50 MHz core, 100 MHz memory, 6.25 MHz pixel
  - APF framework (apf_top, bridge, pad controller) and the command handler
  - the core's own video timing and framebuffer RTL is instantiated

What does not work yet:
  - the picture. The framebuffer's line RAMs are optimised away by the fitter,
    so video output should be assumed blank. See docs/FEASIBILITY.md section 6.
  - the R4600, DCS audio, ATA disk and the memory bridge are not wired up

The measured blocker for the full core is block RAM, not game memory: the
framebuffer alone is 2.46 Mbit of the Pocket's 3.15 Mbit, and the upstream core
needs 113% of the device as it stands. docs/FEASIBILITY.md has the numbers.

Needs, on the SD card (once a working bitstream exists):
  Assets/killerinstinct/common/kinst.rom    (built with tools/build_rom.py)
  Assets/killerinstinct/common/kinst.img    (chdman extracthd from kinst.chd)
  same for kinst2.

ROMs and disk images are NOT included: they are copyrighted Midway/Rare
content. Build the .rom from your own kinst.zip / kinst2.zip dumps.
