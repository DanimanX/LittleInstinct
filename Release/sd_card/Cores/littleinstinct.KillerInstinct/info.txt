Killer Instinct / Killer Instinct 2 for Analogue Pocket (openFPGA).

Port of the arcade core Arcade-KillerInstinct_MiSTer (Midway KI hardware:
R4600 MIPS III 64/32-bit + DCS audio with ADSP-2105 + ATA IDE streaming).

Architecture:
- Target: Analogue Pocket (Intel Cyclone V 5CEBA4F23C8N, 49k LEs)
- CPU: MIPS R4600 trimmed for fixed-point arcade execution (no FPU, ADDR32_ONLY, 32-bit MLAB GPRs)
- Memory: Unified 64 MB SDRAM arbiter for Low RAM, Main RAM, Boot ROM, DCS Sound ROM and Framebuffer
- Video: 320x240 @ 60Hz double framebuffer in SDRAM with 2x M10K line-buffer scanout
- Sound: Midway DCS audio DSP (ADSP-2105) running at 31.25 kHz with 16-bit stereo output over I2S
- Storage: ATA IDE streaming emulation backed by APF Deferred Data Slot 2 (microSD)

Required Assets on MicroSD card:
  Assets/killerinstinct/common/kinst.rom    (Killer Instinct 1 ROM, 4.5 MB)
  Assets/killerinstinct/common/kinst.img    (Killer Instinct 1 CHD sector image, ~130 MB)
  Assets/killerinstinct/common/kinst2.rom   (Killer Instinct 2 ROM, 4.5 MB)
  Assets/killerinstinct/common/kinst2.img   (Killer Instinct 2 CHD sector image, ~140 MB)

ROMs and disk images are NOT included: they are copyrighted Midway/Rare
content. Build the .rom from your own MAME dumps using tools/build_rom.py.
